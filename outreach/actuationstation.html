How this connects to our research

Real soft robots in our lab are made from electroactive polymers — materials that bend or squeeze when electricity is applied, just like the pneumatic and electromagnetic robots in this game. Unlike rigid surgical tools, soft actuators can grip delicate tissue without crushing it.

We use similar principles to design drug delivery systems that release medication only when needed — modulating the dose in response to the biological environment, rather than releasing everything at once.

Actuation Station

Use a robot arm to grab biological payloads and deliver them to the wound. Make 8 deliveries to heal it completely.

Three robot types — rigid, soft pneumatic, and electromagnetic — each works differently. Fibroblasts drift, so you'll need to intercept them. Crush a cell and it respawns. Each delivery teaches you something real about soft robotics.
