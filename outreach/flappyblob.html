How this connects to our research

In our lab, materials must adapt to their biological surroundings. A gel implanted near a wound site needs mechanical properties that match the surrounding tissue — too stiff and it damages cells; too soft and it falls apart.

Flappy Blob exaggerates this challenge: can you adapt your control strategy as the environment changes? The sliders represent real parameters — stiffness, elasticity, viscosity — that our materials scientists tune in the lab to get just the right behaviour.
